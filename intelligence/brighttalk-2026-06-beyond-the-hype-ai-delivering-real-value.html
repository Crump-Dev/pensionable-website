Beyond the Hype: AI Delivering Real Value in Pension Schemes

03 June 2026 — Mark Crump & David Rich · PMI BrightTALK · 55 mins

Click to watch on BrightTALK · Hosted by the Pensions Management Institute

Mark Crump CEO · Pensionable.ai
David Rich CDO · PensionAble.ai · MM

PRT calculation work has historically taken months, or even years, and often involved significant costs for pension schemes. By combining data expertise with AI capabilities across benefit audits, calculations and revaluations, organisations are finding new ways to improve efficiency, reduce costs and increase transparency.

Join MM and Pensionable.ai for a joint webinar exploring how data and AI are being applied across a range of pension scheme activities. The session will focus on practical examples and measurable outcomes, moving beyond speculation to examine where AI is delivering value today.
